Child safety standards

sowocial, by ow-ops · Last updated 13 November 2026

ow-ops has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Using sowocial to create, share, request or promote such content, or to groom or exploit children, is prohibited.

How sowocial works

sowocial is a client for Mastodon, GoToSocial and Bluesky. ow-ops does not host accounts or posts. Content is stored and moderated by the server each account belongs to, under that server's rules and the law where it operates.

Reporting in the app

  • Report a post or an account from its menu. The report goes to the moderators of your server, and on Mastodon and GoToSocial it can also be forwarded to the moderators of the other account's server.
  • Block or mute an account, or block a whole server, to stop seeing it.

Reporting to ow-ops and to the authorities

To report child safety concerns about sowocial itself, contact contact@ow-ops.eu. We review reports promptly, take action where we can, and report CSAM to the relevant authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the national hotline in the country concerned.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police. You can also report CSAM directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline or to a hotline listed by INHOPE.

Compliance

ow-ops complies with applicable child safety laws and cooperates with law enforcement.
