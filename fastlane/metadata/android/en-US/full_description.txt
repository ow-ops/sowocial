sowocial is a GoToSocial and Bluesky client by ow-ops. It also works with Mastodon and other servers that implement the Mastodon client API.

You can sign in to fediverse and Bluesky accounts side by side and read both in one mixed home timeline.

On fediverse servers you can:
- Read home, local and hashtag timelines and threads.
- View profiles and follow or unfollow.
- Read notifications and accept or reject follow requests.
- Write posts with a content warning, a visibility and up to four images with alt text.
- Boost, favourite and bookmark.
